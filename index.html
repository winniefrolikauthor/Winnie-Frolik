The Author

The Author Behind the Mysteries

Born and raised in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Winnie Frolik has always found a home among books. Her lifelong love of reading eventually led her back to writing, where she has created stories filled with mystery, history, complex relationships, and unforgettable outsiders.
