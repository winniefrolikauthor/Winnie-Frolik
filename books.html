The Mary Grey Mysteries   |   Book One

The Illhenny Murders

2021

District nurse Mary Grey saves architect Anthony West after a car wreck, only to hear him insist that the crash was no accident. When Anthony later dies, Mary begins asking questions and finds herself drawn into a dangerous investigation alongside exiled Jewish German detective Franz Schaefer.

The Mary Grey Mysteries   |   Book Two

Death at Bayard Lodge

2022

Mary Grey and her lover Harriet visit Harriet's godmother in the Lake District, but their peaceful visit is shattered when newlywed Rachel Florry is found dead with her skull smashed. As Mary and Franz Schaefer investigate, hidden relationships and dangerous secrets begin to emerge.

The Mary Grey Mysteries   |   Book Three

A Swing of the Axe

2023

When one of Mary's old nursing-school colleagues is murdered, Mary and private detective Franz Schaefer travel north to investigate. The murdered nurse had alerted Scotland Yard to a suspected crime, and the village they enter appears to be hiding more than one secret.

The Mary Grey Mysteries   |   Book Four

Peril in Provence

2024

Mary hears that Harriet West has been arrested for murder in the French town of Munier. Mary travels to Provence, where Harriet confesses to the killing but claims she acted in self-defense. As Mary searches for the truth, more disturbing secrets come to light.

The Mary Grey Mysteries   |   Book Five

Gold, Frankincense, and Morphine

2025

In December 1938, Mary is working at St. Stephen's Hospital in Rosby when a patient dies following what should have been a routine operation. Mary begins to suspect that something darker is happening. As the bodies begin to mount, Franz investigates while Harriet takes in a child refugee arriving through the Kindertransport.
