Upcoming Event October 21–25, 2026

Bouchercon 2026 — Calgary, Alberta

Winnie will be attending Bouchercon 2026 in Calgary, Alberta, where she will be part of the Author Fair, meeting readers, signing books, and discussing the world of the Mary Grey Mysteries with fans old and new.

Winnie will also be sitting on a panel discussing writing characters with depth, where she will share her approach to bringing Mary Grey and the rich cast of the series to life on the page.

Author Fair Details
  • Date: Friday, October 23, 2026
  • Time: 7:00–8:30 PM
  • Location: McLeod D Hall

Stop by to say hello, browse the full Mary Grey Mysteries series, and pick up a signed copy. Winnie would love to meet you.
