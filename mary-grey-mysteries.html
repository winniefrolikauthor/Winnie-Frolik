About the Series

Mary Grey, District Nurse

At the heart of the series is Mary Grey, a district nurse whose sharp mind and tenacious spirit draw her into mysteries far beyond her medical duties. Set against the gathering darkness of prewar Europe and the turbulence of the Second World War, the Mary Grey mysteries blend human drama, historical atmosphere, and compelling investigation. Alongside Franz Schaefer, an exiled Jewish German detective, and her beloved Harriet, Mary navigates a world of secrets, danger, and moral complexity.
